XIAO ESP32-C6 -> RC522 / buzzer wiring

XIAO 3V3      -> RC522 3.3V
XIAO GND      -> RC522 GND
XIAO D8/GPIO19  -> RC522 SCK
XIAO D9/GPIO20  -> RC522 MISO
XIAO D10/GPIO18 -> RC522 MOSI
XIAO D3/GPIO21  -> RC522 SDA / SS / CS
XIAO D2/GPIO2   -> RC522 RST
XIAO D1/GPIO1   -> existing buzzer/speaker signal

Do not use GPIO3 or GPIO14 for peripherals in this build: they control the
XIAO ESP32-C6 antenna RF switch when USE_EXTERNAL_ANTENNA=true.

RC522 is a 3.3V device. Do not power its logic from 5V.
